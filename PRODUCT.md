# Product

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineers looking to hire or collaborate with an AI/ML Engineer and Data Analyst. They want a fast, high-end, and frictionless experience to view past work, skills, and resume.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio website that showcases the expertise of M. Wajeeh in Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and Generative AI. It needs to establish immediate credibility, convey a sense of premium quality (editorial/luxury style), and drive contacts/resume downloads.
