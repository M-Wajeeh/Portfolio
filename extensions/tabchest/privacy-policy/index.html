TabChest privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · TabChest v1.0.2 · 100% Local-First

What data TabChest uses

TabChest uses the following browser data only to provide its core features:

  • Open tab metadata (URL, title, favicon, pinned state) to save and restore workspaces.
  • Extension settings and workspace data stored locally in your browser.

Where your data is stored

  • Workspace data is stored locally on your device (IndexedDB and extension storage).
  • TabChest does not run a backend that stores your browsing history or workspace data.

Data sharing

TabChest does not sell your data.

TabChest only sends data to a third party in one optional case:

  • If you activate TabChest Pro, your license key (format: TABCHEST-XXXX) and an anonymous device identifier are sent to Polar (https://api.polar.sh/*) to validate your purchase (enforcing a 3-device limit).

No tab URLs, tab titles, or workspace contents are ever sent for license validation.

Permissions explained

tabs
Required to read selected tab info (URL, title, pinned state) so workspaces can be saved and restored.
storage
Required to save your workspaces and preferences locally in IndexedDB and extension storage.
alarms
Used for optional weekly backup reminders in Pro and periodic maintenance tasks locally.
notifications
Used to show alerts when automated weekly backup reminders trigger.
favicon
Required to display site icons next to saved tabs for quick visual recognition.
contextMenus
Adds right-click options to save active tabs or windows directly to a workspace.
https://api.polar.sh/*
Used strictly to verify optional TABCHEST-XXXX Pro license keys and device activations (3-device limit).

Your choices

  • You can delete any workspace at any time from the popup.
  • You can export your workspaces to JSON and keep an offline backup.
  • You can remove your license key from settings.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all local extension storage data.
