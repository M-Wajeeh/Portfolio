Smart Reader privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Smart Reader v0.3.0 · 100% Local-First

Overview & Core Guarantee

Smart Reader does not collect, track, sell, or send your data anywhere.

All article extraction, reading views, and AI summaries execute directly on your computer. Zero page text or personal notes are uploaded to any remote server.

Data & Privacy Practices

  • On-demand page access: Smart Reader only reads a page when you explicitly click its icon, use its shortcut, or choose it from the right-click menu.
  • Local on-device summaries: Generated on your device, either by Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano) or by a local algorithm. Page content is never sent to any server.
  • What is stored locally: Articles you choose to save (title, link, site name, reading time, summary) and your highlights. These are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage. Your reader settings (theme, font size) use Chrome sync storage so they follow your Chrome profile.
  • License checks (Pro only): If you activate Pro, your license key (format: SMARTREADER-XXXX) and an anonymous device ID are sent to Polar (https://api.polar.sh/*) to confirm it is valid (enforcing a 3-device limit). No article URLs, titles, highlights, or summaries are ever shared.
  • No tracking: Zero analytics, zero ads, and zero third-party telemetry apart from the optional license check above.
  • Deleting data: Delete items from the library at any time, or remove the extension to erase everything.

Permissions explained

activeTab
Grants access to extract article text on the active tab only when you deliberately open Smart Reader.
storage
Required to store saved articles, highlights, and reader preferences locally in your browser.
contextMenus
Provides a convenient right-click shortcut to open the current page in reader view.
https://api.polar.sh/*
Used strictly to verify optional SMARTREADER-XXXX Pro license keys and manage the 3-device limit.
