# Design System

## Register
brand

## Pattern
Portfolio Grid (Editorial Masonry)

## Style
Motion-Driven. Animation-heavy, microinteractions, smooth transitions, scroll effects, parallax, entrance animations. Monochrome + Gold accent.

## Theme & Lighting
Dark mode default. A recruiter reviewing the portfolio on a MacBook in a well-lit office, or an engineer scrolling on their phone late at night. The high contrast provides a pocket-manifesto, luxury reading experience.

## Colors
- **Primary Background**: `#1C1917` (Premium Dark)
- **Primary Foreground**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary Background**: `#292524`
- **Accent**: `#A16207` (Gold)
- **Accent Hover**: `#CA8A04`
- **Muted Text**: `#A8A29E`
- **Borders**: `#44403C`

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Playfair Display`, serif. Bold, editorial, austere.
- **Body**: `Source Serif 4` and `Inter`, sans-serif.
- **Monospace/Accents**: `JetBrains Mono` for section numbers (`01 // ORIGINS`).

## Layout & Motion
- No nested cards. Use spacing and typography for rhythm.
- Section titles with serif fonts.
- Intersection observer scroll animations. Easing out with exponential curves.
